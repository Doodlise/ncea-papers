Accessibility

This site aims to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. It uses plain HTML, high-contrast text, visible keyboard focus, semantic headings and landmarks, and works at mobile widths and without JavaScript (use the plain list).

Assessed against WCAG 2.2 AA in November 2026, including automated checks and manual keyboard and screen-reader-oriented review. Known limitations: very long NZQA document titles may need horizontal scrolling on a 320px-wide screen, and some past papers are scanned PDFs that may not be fully readable by screen readers.
