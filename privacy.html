Privacy

This site collects no personal information. There are no cookies, analytics, advertising, forms or account logins, and nothing you type here is sent to us.

Your browser remembers your last selected standard in local storage on your own device (ncea-papers-last) so it can reopen where you left off. You can clear it at any time through your browser's site-data settings.

When you follow a PDF link you leave this site and go to NZQA, whose privacy statement then applies. The hosting provider logs IP addresses for security as described in its privacy statement.
